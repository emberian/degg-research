# Research Workstreams

## W0 — Semantic clearing oracle

Freeze Clearing V0, exact allocation, tie-break, and refusals. Deliver exhaustive
small-domain vectors and two independent implementations.

## W1 — Dark Relation IR

Specify bounded vectors, scans, crossing, selection, allocation, conservation,
visibility, and receipt denotations. No backend code until the denotation is
reviewed.

## W2 — Discrete CKKS bakeoff

Reproduce the exact-integer claims of selected discrete CKKS and SIMD ALU papers
on the clearing operators. Compare to clear and TFHE baselines.

## W3 — Hybrid BFV plus nonlinear boundary

Rebuild a clean, provenance-safe experiment for additive book aggregation plus
MPC/proof crossing. Close the multi-process and malicious-input story before a
Dark claim.

## W4 — Verifiable computation

Compare result certificate, SNARK/STARK of relation, proof of FHE evaluation,
replicated evaluation, and optimistic dispute. Identify the smallest proof that
onchain settlement actually needs.

## W5 — Private local outputs and settlement

Deliver per-owner fills without publishing the fill vector. Model public SPL,
Token-2022 confidential transfer, shielded notes, and hybrid settlement leakage.

## W6 — Inclusion, availability, and censorship

Design a canonical batch root, inclusion receipt, non-equivocation, data
availability, timeout, and refund relation.

## W7 — Guarded holes

Build the factual matrix distinguishing ordinary callbacks, service escrow,
funded contingent claims, tradable holes, and venue-listed contracts. Produce
technical regulator questions without legal conclusion laundering.

## W8 — Regulatory observability

Study encrypted records, bounded compliance predicates, credentials, selective
disclosure, public integrity proofs, and emergency powers. Preserve the
Clear/Shielded/Dark taxonomy.

## W9 — Accelerator mapping

Map relation operators to CPU SIMD, GPU, FPGA, and external TPU research. Measure
residency and communication rather than kernel time alone.

## W10 — Provenance and reproducibility

Create artifact manifests, dependency/license review, patent boundary, clean
specification process, and reproducible benchmark harness.

## W11 — Public research communication

Maintain claim ledger, negative results, technical explainers, CFTC public
comment, meeting appendix, and publishable research papers.
