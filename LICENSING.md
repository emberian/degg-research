## Artifact movement

An artifact crossing into or out of this repository requires:

- source repository and immutable commit;
- exact source paths;
- license and copyright;
- third-party derivation;
- generator command;
- canonical bytes and SHA-256;
- receiver checks.

License compatibility does not replace provenance.
